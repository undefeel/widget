## Target
Prevent AILimits termination when a child closes stdin, bound subprocess cleanup,
and accept valid Codex JSON-RPC reply layouts. Audit F-06/F-07/F-11.

## Change
Read Sources/App/Fetchers.swift, especially Shell.run (11–49), CLI.find (74),
and CodexFetcher.fetchCLI (234–270), plus plan/specs/01-01.review.md.
Keep the current Shell.run interface and output/error contract unless a minimal
internal helper is needed. Use safe throwing writes and per-descriptor SIGPIPE
protection on macOS (prefer F_SETNOSIGPIPE over changing process-global signals).
Handle child stdin closure, launch failure, timeout, early stop and EOF without
crashing, leaking handlers/handles or leaving a terminated child unreaped. Avoid
unbounded writes that outlive the requested timeout. Preserve complete stdout
before ordinary exit and ensure early-stop captures the matching full line.

CLI.find must use a positional shell argument (command -v "$1") instead of
interpolating a command name. Preserve zsh login lookup and current search dirs.
Codex reply matching must parse complete newline-delimited JSON objects and
compare the top-level numeric request ID 1, regardless of key order/whitespace.
Ignore partial trailing lines, unrelated IDs/notifications and malformed lines;
handle both result and error replies. Do not change upstream protocol methods,
provider auth, fallback policy or call real agents during tests.
