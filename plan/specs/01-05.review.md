## Follow-up error-path finding

- Orchestrator finding: a failed `sqlite3_open_v2` could leave its error handle
  in `db`; the failable initializer then closed it and deinitialization closed
  the same handle again.
- Disposition: after closing on open failure, clear `db`; deinitialization now
  closes only a non-nil owned handle. The focused harness instruments the
  extracted production reader's close call, counts one effective close, and
  suppresses any unexpected repeated native close so it never calls SQLite with
  a freed handle.
